# Supporters

mcDonald is free and open source, and it will stay that way. The people on this page help make that possible. Thank
you.

[Donate to the mcDonald UAP Toolkit](https://www.zeffy.com/en-US/donation-form/project-janus-mcdonald-uap-toolkit){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

Gifts go through Blue Marble Space, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and are tax-deductible as allowed by law.

## How your name is listed

Every gift helps, and a gift of $25 or more puts your name on this page. Larger gifts, or gifts made every month,
also put your name in our journal articles and in the program itself.

| | One-time gift | Monthly gift | Your name appears |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Supporter** | $25 or more | $5 or more | On this page |
| **Acknowledged** | $100 or more | $10 or more | On this page, and in the acknowledgments of our journal articles |
| **Patron** | $1,000 or more | $100 or more | On this page, in our journal articles, and in "About mcDonald" in the program |

- **How long.** A one-time gift is listed for 12 months. A monthly gift is listed for as long as it continues.
- **Journal articles.** We name donors in the acknowledgments of journal articles that build or use mcDonald,
  submitted while they are listed. A journal article's acknowledgments are part of the scientific record.
- **The program.** Patrons are named under Help → About mcDonald in every version released while they are listed.
  Each version is archived on [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23652954), so your name stays in those
  versions permanently.
- **Founding Supporters.** Our first 100 donors, at any level, are marked as Founding Supporters on this page
  permanently.
- **Your choice.** You choose the name we show, or to stay anonymous. To change how you are listed, write to
  [jacob@bmsis.org](mailto:jacob@bmsis.org). We review every name before it is listed.

## Our promise

Donors have no role in what mcDonald measures, which videos we study, or what our results say. A gift supports the
toolkit; it never buys an answer.
