# Publications

The papers mcdonald was built to reproduce. Both are in review, and the preprints are free to read on arXiv.

## Limits on Kinematic Inference from the PURSUE Airborne Sensor Videos

Haqq-Misra, J. & Kopparapu, R. (2026). Limits on Kinematic Inference from the PURSUE Airborne Sensor Videos.
*Journal of Aerospace Information Systems* (AIAA), Technical Note, in review.
[arXiv:2608.12445](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12445)

What a single camera's video can and cannot say about how fast an object moves. mcdonald's report follows the
paper's framework, and the package's tests check that it gives the paper's numbers.

[Read the preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12445){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

## Six objects reported at 480 mph in a declassified military infrared video show brightness flicker at bird wingbeat rates

Haqq-Misra, J., Kopparapu, R., Cloete, R., Eldadi, O., Shermer, M. & Loeb, A. (2026). Six objects reported at
480 mph in a declassified military infrared video show brightness flicker at bird wingbeat rates.
*Scientific Reports*, in review. [arXiv:2610.08692](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08692).
Reproduction package: [doi:10.5281/zenodo.23021686](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23021686)

The six objects' brightness beats at the rate of a bird's wingbeat. mcdonald can measure the beat in any video
the way this paper does.

[Read the preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08692){ .md-button .md-button--primary }
[Reproduction package](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23021686){ .md-button }
