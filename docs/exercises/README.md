# Exercises

Short exercises for learning **mcdonald** on real videos. Each one says what to do in the window
and on the command line, and what answer you should get, so you can check your own work. They
use the public PURSUE videos, which mcdonald downloads for you by name.

You need **mcdonald** installed and set up first: see [Install](../../README.md#install).

| | Exercise | You learn | Download |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Your first video (PR23)](01-pr23.md) | open a video by name, press one button, and read the report | 5 MB |

## Tell us how it went

When you finish an exercise, please
[send us your result](https://github.com/haqqmisra/mcdonald/issues/new?template=exercise-result.yml),
whether or not it matched. It takes a couple of minutes, and you need a free GitHub account.
A result that matched tells us mcdonald works on your computer. One that did not, or a step
that was hard to follow, tells us what to fix.
