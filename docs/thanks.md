# Thank you

Thank you for your gift to the mcDonald UAP Toolkit. It helps keep mcDonald free and open source for everyone who
wants to study UAP videos carefully.

## What happens next

- **Your receipt** comes by email from Zeffy, which handles gifts for Blue Marble Space, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit.
- **Your name** goes on our [Supporters](supporters.md) page when we next update it, about once a month, unless you
  chose to give anonymously. If you are one of our first 20 donors, you are also marked as a Founding Supporter,
  permanently.
- **To change how you are listed,** write to [jacob@bmsis.org](mailto:jacob@bmsis.org).
