# About

**mcdonald** is created by [Jacob Haqq Misra](https://www.haqqmisra.net/) as part of [Project Janus](https://janus.bluemarblespace.org/) at [Blue Marble Space](https://www.bluemarblespace.org/). It is developed in support of the work of the [UAP Science Advisory Council](https://uapsac.com/) and the [Galileo Project](https://galileo.hsites.harvard.edu/), both of which study unidentified anomalous phenomena (UAP). The toolkit is fully deterministic: the same video and the same marks give the same numbers every time. It is meant for studying UAP videos with the same rigor as any other problem in science.

Questions, problems, and results that look wrong are welcome as [issues on GitHub](https://github.com/haqqmisra/mcdonald/issues). See the [Contribute](contributing.md) page for details. A video of your own can be sent for analysis from the [Submit](submit.md) page.

## James McDonald

<!-- README: quote -->

James McDonald (1920-1971) was a professor of meteorology at the University of Arizona and a member of the National Academy of Sciences. He argued for serious scientific study of UFOs, which today are called UAP, and he carried out his own investigations of Project Blue Book's unexplained cases. His paper ["Science in Default"](https://www.nicap.org/papers/mcdonald/Science%20in%20Default-Twenty-Two_Years_aaas_69.pdf) remains a template for approaching UAP with the curiosity and scrutiny of a scientist.
